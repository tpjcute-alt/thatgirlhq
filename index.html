Urge Log
The Habit Reframe Method.
a system for reclaiming your attention and motivation — honestly.
📖 Indistractable — Nir Eyal
📖 Stolen Focus — Johann Hari
The Curve
The 7 Phases
1
Identity: Tech Sober
Decide who you are. You are someone who is not controlled by technology or impulses. This is identity-level — not a rule, not a restriction. You simply are that person now. Say it: "I am tech sober."
2
Cold Turkey
Don't rely on app blockers — you can use them if they're genuinely hard to unblock, but the point is to say no to yourself, not outsource willpower to software. If it feels too hard at the start, tier down your hobbies — books, podcasts, walks instead of Reddit or Reels. Lower stimulation, not zero stimulation.
3
Meditate
Sit with yourself. You don't need an app. Even 5 minutes of stillness. The goal is to reduce baseline stimulation so your nervous system can settle into focus. Link to your meditate tab →
3.5
Observe Feelings (Do Nothing)
Sit with boredom. Don't escape it, don't fix it, just notice it. This is where the magic happens — your brain learns that discomfort is survivable. If it's really too much, allow a Tier 3 vice (books, podcasts, slow content) rather than the high-stimulation stuff.
4
Ideal Dose
Set the conditions and context for when you'll allow vices. Some people do 1 full day of vices per week rather than a little every day — because a little every day drains willpower constantly. Decide your version. The key: it's planned, not reactive.
Your Ideal Dose Plan
5
Ramp Up & Binge
You faltered. It happened. Now — blame the vice, not yourself. This is crucial and it's honest: the vice is engineered to be addictive. You're not weak. Pinning helps here: after you slip, say out loud "that thing pulled me in. It's designed to." Don't spiral into shame — redirect.
6
Reset — Ask Yourself These Questions
After a slip, journal through these honestly:
💭 What actually happened? Was I demotivated or overwhelmed, yet expecting myself to plow through heavy tasks?
🌀 Did the vice seem reasonable at first — just a small break — but then spiral out of control?
⚡ Was there a stressor or trigger during the work session, even a subtle one, that pushed me to escape?
Journal your reset
7
Productivity Burst ✦
After a few cycles, you'll feel a burst of genuine motivation. When it comes — just work until you want to stop. Do not push beyond it. Do not force yourself to continue or you'll create an expectancy gap and start associating work with negative emotions.

When you take a break at the start of it, do pinning: say to yourself "Yes! That felt lovely to do that work." Anchor the good feeling. You're retraining your brain to see focused work as a reward, not a punishment.
Pinning reminder:
"Yes — that felt good. I did the work. That's who I am."
Your Vice Tiers
Categorise your vices by how stimulating they are. Tier down gradually, not cold turkey to nothing.
🔴 Tier 1 — High Stimulation
Reddit, TikTok, Instagram scrolling, YouTube rabbit holes…
🟡 Tier 2 — Medium
YouTube vlogs, casual games, long-form social content…
🟢 Tier 3 — Low Stimulation
Books, podcasts, walks, slow cooking shows…
